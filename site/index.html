    How it works. Google Trends reports interest on a relative 0–100 scale. We fetch five years of weekly data plus the last 90 days daily, rescale the daily figures onto the weekly scale, then compare the last seven days with the average height of past Christmas peaks. That ratio is the Festivity Index: 100 means we’re as Mariah-mad as a typical Christmas week.
