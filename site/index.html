    How it works

    Google Trends scores search interest from 0 to 100. Each morning we fetch five years of weekly figures and the last 90 days of daily figures for “All I Want for Christmas Is You” in the country, and put both on the same scale. The Festivity Index is the past week’s average as a share of a typical Christmas-week peak. Snowfall on this page is proportional to it.
